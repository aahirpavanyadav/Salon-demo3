### 3. Website Architecture

1. **Editorial Masthead (Navbar):**
   - Clean wordmark with subtitle `L'Atelier de Beauté • Hyderabad`
   - Sticky frosted glass effect with ivory backdrop
   - One-tap booking CTA & WhatsApp Concierge toggle
   - Animated full-screen magazine overlay menu on mobile

2. **Hero Section (Editorial Masterpiece):**
   - Asymmetric split layout
   - Headline: *"THE ART OF LOOKING AND FEELING BEAUTIFUL."*
   - Supporting copy: *"Discover a space where beauty, confidence and personal expression come together."*
   - High-resolution editorial photography with custom curtain reveal animation
   - Dual CTAs: *Discover Our Services* & *Book Your Visit*

3. **Brand Story (Magazine Manifesto):**
   - Headline: *"BEAUTY IS AN ART. MAKE IT YOURS."*
   - Story of the Gudimalkapur Hyderabad atelier
   - 3 Atelier Pillars: Precision Artistry, Hospital-Grade Disinfection, and Unhurried Luxury

4. **Services (The Editorial Collection):**
   - Non-card, alternating magazine spreads
   - Category filtering: Hair Couture, Skin Radiance, Haute Bridal, Hand & Foot Spa
   - Editorial numbers, detailed descriptions, verified starting prices, and direct booking links

5. **The Experience (The Photographic Spaces):**
   - Photographic showcase of the salon's distinct environments:
     - 01 — The Styling Suite
     - 02 — The Private Bridal Chamber
     - 03 — The Wash & Scalp Sanctuary
     - 04 — The Skin & Radiance Lab
   - Interactive space switcher and filmstrip preview

6. **Signature Moments (Interactive Transformation):**
   - Interactive Before/After slider with smooth horizontal drag handle
   - Case study dossiers on Balayage & Silk Finish + Porcelain Glass Skin Rejuvenation

7. **The Beauty Journal (Fashion Photography Gallery):**
   - Asymmetric masonry grid with filter tabs
   - Full-screen luxury lightbox with smooth modal animations, captions, and keyboard navigation (Esc, Arrow keys)

8. **Client Testimonials (Vogue Pull-Quotes):**
   - Authentic Google Business Profile reviews
   - 4.9★ rating certification with reviewer name and treatment details

9. **Location & Rendezvous:**
   - Split layout with verified address, operating hours, direct phone, and directions guide
   - Embedded interactive Google Maps location view
   - Interactive FAQ accordion for guest inquiries

10. **Atelier Concierge (WhatsApp AI Receptionist):**
    - Redesigned in Warm Editorial Luxury theme
    - Floating concierge pill with live status badge
    - Conversational assistant "Geneviève" answering questions on pricing, services, timings, and location
    - Dynamic "Continue in WhatsApp" deep link generator pre-filling the guest's enquiry

11. **Appointment Reservation Modal:**
    - Interactive booking flow with ritual selector, date/time picker, guest details, and confetti celebration
    - Direct "Send Confirmation to WhatsApp" action
